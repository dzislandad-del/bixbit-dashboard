## Verdict: UNCHANGED — Classical Local SEO NOT APPLICABLE

No material change since June. Re-verified `/en/contacts`, `/en/about`, `/en/global-presence`,
and the fresh `data/site-structure.md` (2026-09-16 crawl). Findings below.

---

## 1. What changed since June

**Nothing structural.** Specifically checked and ruled out:

- **New region/country landing pages** (`/en/usa`, `/en/kazakhstan`, etc.): **none found.**
  `data/site-structure.md` (re-crawled today, 28 URLs spot-checked + 5 sitemaps) lists no
  country- or region-specific landing pages anywhere in the site tree.
- **`/en/global-presence`**: still **HTTP 404**. Not built.
- **`/en/about`**: still describes "three global offices" (Europe, North America, South Asia)
  but only **two** addresses are ever given, and the "third" office is never named or located.
  No street address disclosed for the Poland HQ (same gap as June).
- **`/en/contacts`**: still no physical address, no data-center location. Only phone/email/
  messaging-bot contacts.
- **USA "office"**: still resolves to **Big Star Mining, 240 Centre Dr., Burleson, TX** — a
  third-party reseller/partner, not a BiXBiT legal entity. Same as June; still not eligible for
  a BiXBiT GBP listing (would violate Google's ownership-verification policy).

## 2. New minor finding (not in June report)

- **Phone number inconsistency**: `/en/contacts` now shows **+48 574 168 486**, while `/en/about`
  still shows **+48 226 020 088** (the number from June). Two different Poland numbers on two
  official pages of the same site. This is a NAP-adjacent inconsistency worth a content-team fix,
  but it does not change the core verdict — there is still no verified street address to anchor a
  GBP/LocalBusiness listing to, regardless of which phone number is correct.

## 3. Verdict rationale (reconfirmed, see June report §3 for full detail)

1. No verified BiXBiT street address publicly disclosed anywhere on the site (still true).
2. Digital-first product (firmware download, cloud SaaS monitoring, shipped hardware) — no
   walk-in location.
3. Global B2B ICP, not local searchers.
4. GBP requires a verifiable physical address with staff present; using the Big Star Mining
   partner address would violate GBP policy and risk suspension.

## 4. Recommendation — unchanged, still open

The June P0/P1 recommendations remain **unimplemented** and still stand as the correct path:
- P0: `Organization` schema with `contactPoint`/`areaServed` (note: `technical-seo`'s 2026-09-16
  crawl confirms `Organization` JSON-LD now exists sitewide, but its `description` field is
  cooling-page ad copy reused verbatim on every page — not the NAP/contact fix intended here;
  hand this to `technical-seo` to correct alongside their JSON-LD cleanup).
- P1: build `/en/global-presence` (still 404) as a trust-signal page naming served regions.
- P1: B2B directory citations (Clutch, G2, Crunchbase) — no address required.
- P2: country/region-specific landing pages for priority markets (US, KZ, PY, AE, LatAm, CIS) —
  still not built, still the recommended substitute for classical local SEO.

## 5. What NOT to do (reconfirmed)
- Do not create a GBP listing using the Big Star Mining address.
- Do not invent an address for the unnamed "third office" or the Poland HQ.
- Do not resolve the phone-number inconsistency by guessing which is correct — flag to the
  client/content owner to confirm the authoritative number.
