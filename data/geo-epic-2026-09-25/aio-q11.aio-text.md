ePIC UMC OS is a custom third-party Bitcoin mining firmware designed to replace stock Bitmain firmware on Antminer S19 and S21 series ASICs.[[1]](https://epicblockchain.io/product/umc-os/)

### 📊 Core Overview

- ⚙️ **Function:** Chip-level tuning, automated optimization, and thermal protection.
- 💰 **Dev Fee:** 1.5% (lowered from 2.5%).
- 🔌 **Hardware Option:** Use firmware-only on stock control boards or buy the physical ePIC UMC board (zero dev fee).
- 🛠️ **Deployment:** Pushed remotely using RigRunner mass-deployment tool.
- 📉 **Supported Models:** Air-cooled Antminer S19j, S19 XP, S19K Pro, T21, and S21 series.[[1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eb30yInbBn0)[[2]](https://epicblockchain.io/product/umc-os/)[[3]](https://epicblockchain.io/custom-antminer-firmware-guide-umc-os-bitcoin-mining-firmware-installation-with-rigrunner/)

### 💡 Key Features

- 📈 **Perpetual Tune:** Dynamically targets peak efficiency and maintains hashrates.
- 🔎 **Granular Controls:** Includes Voltage Optimizer, Board Tune, and Chip Tune.
- 🗒️ **API Support:** Integrates directly with standard management software and internal systems.[[1]](https://epicblockchain.io/epic-blockchain-technologies-reduces-umc-os-development-fee-to-1-5-extending-institutional-grade-optimization-across-mining-fleets/)[[2]](https://epicblockchain.io/product/umc-os/)[[3]](https://epicblockchain.io/custom-antminer-firmware-guide-umc-os-bitcoin-mining-firmware-installation-with-rigrunner/)[[4]](https://epicblockchain.io/product/)

### 🗓️ Installation Steps

- 📥 Download the software from the ePIC Firmware Downloads page and grab RigRunner from GitHub.
- 🔎 Scan your local network to find your miner's IP address (`cminer` label).
- 💻 Run RigRunner, select "Update Firmware," and load the downloaded ZIP file.
- ⚙️ Enter your pool details, worker configurations, and desired hashrate target.[[1]](https://epicblockchain.io/product/umc-os/)

If you share your **specific miner model** (e.g., S21 or S19K Pro) and **current setup** (stock board vs. ePIC control board), I can provide exact configuration presets and tuning parameters.
