Using specialized or updated firmware can reduce your Antminer's power consumption by lowering the voltage delivered to the hash boards, though it also results in a proportional drop in hashrate.[[1]](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=4942283.0)

### How Firmware Reduces Power

- **Underclocking / Downvolting:** Custom or official low-power profiles reduce the electrical voltage sent to the chips, directly cutting wattage drawn from the wall.[[1]](https://www.asicclub.com.ua/en/firmware-antminer-s19-pro-plus-hydro/)[[2]](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=4942283.0)[[3]](https://www.zeusbtc.com/articles/asic-miner-troubleshooting/1171-instructions-of-new-multi-option-firmware)
- **Per-Chip Auto-Tuning:** Advanced third-party firmware analyzes individual ASIC chips to find the most energy-efficient frequency and voltage configuration, maximizing Joules-per-terahash efficiency.[[1]](https://obm.io/blog/miners-guide-to-firmware/)[[2]](https://bixbit.io/en/blog/post/save-unprofitable-asic-equipment)
- **AsicBoost Technology:** Built-in optimization features improve computing efficiency, which can lower relative power consumption per unit of work.[[1]](https://www.asicclub.com.ua/en/firmware-antminer-s19-pro-plus-hydro/)

### Options for Antminer Firmware

- **Official Bitmain Firmware:** Newer models (like the S21 or L7 ) often feature built-in low-power modes or official updates via the dashboard's "Firmware upgrade" menu that optimize efficiency safely.[[1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D6Fg69J2s4M)[[2]](https://www.ydminer.com/news/bitmain-antminer-s21-profitability-2/)
- **Third-Party Custom Firmware:** Platforms like [NiceHash Firmware](https://www.nicehash.com/firmware) , Hiveon , or Luxor provide advanced tuning profiles, auto-scaling, and granular power-target settings for popular models like the S19 or L3+ series.[[1]](https://www.reddit.com/r/NiceHash/comments/1n5mcsk/with_our_custom_asic_firmware_you_can_boost/)[[2]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QQHSnPaNn_c)[[3]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bnNBAwVwE04)

### Risks and Considerations

- **Hashrate Tradeoff:** Lower power consumption always means a lower total hashrate, so you must calculate if the electricity savings outweigh the loss in coin production.[[1]](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=4942283.0)
- **Dev Fees:** Most third-party custom firmware charges a small developer fee (usually 1% to 3% of your mining time/hashrate).[[1]](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=4942283.0)
- **Hardware Stability:** Aggressive manual under-volting can cause hardware errors or system instability if chips are pushed below their viable operating threshold.[[1]](https://voskcointalk.com/t/tuning-l3-asic-miner-for-best-performance-or-power-savings-hw-errors-etc/28316)
