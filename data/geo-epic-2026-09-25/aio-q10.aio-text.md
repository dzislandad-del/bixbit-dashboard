### Third-Party / Aftermarket Firmware

- **Popular Options:** Custom firmware solutions like [Braiins OS](https://braiins.com/blog/smarter-clock-speed-management-for-bitcoin-miners-maximize-roi-minimize-waste), LuxOS , and [VNISH](https://vnish.pro/en/firmware-antminer-s19-hydro.html) offer advanced tuning and precise power-target controls.[[1]](https://bitcoin.stackexchange.com/questions/122121/how-to-remotely-configure-antminer-s19-pro-hash-rate-or-ideally-power-limit-in)[[2]](https://hashrateindex.com/blog/how-luxos-can-maximize-your-asic-miners-power-consumption-and-revenue/)[[3]](https://vnish.pro/en/firmware-antminer-s19-hydro.html)[[4]](https://braiins.com/blog/smarter-clock-speed-management-for-bitcoin-miners-maximize-roi-minimize-waste)
- **Undervolting & Autotuning:** These tools let you set a specific wattage target or percentage reduction by granularly tuning chip frequencies and voltages per individual hashboard.[[1]](https://braiins.com/blog/smarter-clock-speed-management-for-bitcoin-miners-maximize-roi-minimize-waste)[[2]](https://bitcoin.stackexchange.com/questions/122121/how-to-remotely-configure-antminer-s19-pro-hash-rate-or-ideally-power-limit-in)[[3]](https://www.miners1688.com/asic-firmware-tuning-and-undervolting-for-peak-efficiency/)
- **Benefits:** They often achieve better efficiency (J/TH) curves than stock firmware, allowing you to maximize profitability when electricity costs spike.[[1]](https://www.miners1688.com/asic-firmware-tuning-and-undervolting-for-peak-efficiency/)[[2]](https://vnish.pro/en/firmware-antminer-s19-hydro.html)
